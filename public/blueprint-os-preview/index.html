Drawing № 07 · FAQ

Frequently pondered.

I'm not technical. Will I mess this up?+

You don't set it up alone. We run a 30-minute setup session with you, then a 30-minute training session so your team knows how to use it.

What's the monthly bill?+

Nothing from us. Blueprint OS does its AI work through your own Claude subscription from Anthropic. If you don't have one yet, we'll help you pick a plan on the setup call.

What happens to my data if Blueprint IT goes away?+

Your vault is plain markdown files in your own Dropbox, iCloud or OneDrive. Open them in any text editor. Nothing about your shop depends on us.

Can my employees break something?+

No. Blueprint OS Chat is read-only. It can look things up and answer questions, but it can't edit or delete your files. Every conversation is saved back to the vault.

I've tried AI before. Why is this different?+

Generic AI knows nothing about your shop. Blueprint OS reads your context first, so the answers come from your own jobs, customers and SOPs.

What if it isn't right for us?+

There's a 30-day refund. If Blueprint OS isn't earning its place, tell us and we'll make it right.
